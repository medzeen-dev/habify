# Purpose

---

# Executive Summary

habify30 is a B2B behaviour change solution that helps organisations increase the transfer of learning into everyday work.

Most organisations invest significant resources in training, workshops and development programmes. While participants often leave these interventions motivated and inspired, only a small proportion of the intended behavioural changes become part of daily work.

habify30 addresses this gap.

Instead of focusing on learning itself, the product provides a structured transfer architecture that supports people in building new work habits over a defined period after a learning intervention.

The objective is not more knowledge.

The objective is measurable behavioural change.

---

# The Problem

Learning alone rarely changes behaviour.

Employees often leave a workshop with clear intentions, but quickly return to established routines once everyday work resumes.

Typical reasons include:

- competing priorities
- lack of reminders
- insufficient reflection
- missing accountability
- limited opportunities to practise
- absence of social support
- no structured follow-up

As a result, organisations frequently fail to realise the intended return on investment of learning initiatives.

---

# The Solution

habify30 extends learning beyond the classroom.

It provides a structured transfer journey of approximately six weeks in total (Ready Check, Impulsphase, Veränderungswerkstatt, Momentum), that helps participants integrate selected behaviours into their daily work. The 30-day figure in the product name refers specifically to the Momentum Phase, not the full journey.

The solution combines:

- structured reflection
- small behavioural experiments
- habit formation
- peer accountability
- regular prompts
- practical application in real work situations

The emphasis is always on implementation rather than information.

# Value Proposition

habify30 helps organisations increase the probability that learning becomes everyday behaviour.

Rather than replacing existing learning solutions, it complements them by providing the missing transfer layer.

---

# Product Scope

habify30 is designed to support behavioural transfer after learning interventions.

It is not intended to replace:

- Learning Management Systems
- e-learning platforms
- coaching
- classroom training
- facilitators

Instead, it connects these interventions with everyday work.

---

# Core Principles

The project is based on several guiding principles.

## Behaviour over knowledge

Learning is valuable only when it results in behavioural change.

---

## Everyday work is the learning environment

The workplace—not the workshop—is where sustainable learning is created.

---

## Small actions outperform large intentions

Behaviour change is achieved through repeated small actions rather than occasional major efforts.

---

## Reflection creates awareness

People are more likely to change behaviour they consciously observe.

---

## Social commitment strengthens implementation

Peer structures increase consistency without relying on external control.

---

## Simplicity increases participation

The product should minimise friction and cognitive load wherever possible.

---

# Transfer Architecture

habify30 is built around a structured transfer journey.

The architecture currently consists of four connected phases:

1. Ready Check
2. Impulsphase (Kickstart)
3. Veränderungswerkstatt (Clarity Lab)
4. Momentum Phase

Each phase serves a different purpose and together they support sustained behavioural implementation.

Detailed descriptions are provided in **03_Product_Architecture.md**.

# Current Status

The overall product concept is well defined.

The transfer architecture has been developed and refined through multiple iterations.

Several components—including the transfer journey, peer approach and behavioural focus—are considered stable.

Some implementation details, content elements and technical decisions continue to evolve.

---

# Success Criteria

habify30 is successful when participants:

- consistently apply selected behaviours
- establish sustainable work habits
- maintain behaviour after the programme ends
- perceive measurable improvement in everyday work

For organisations, success means improved transfer of learning and greater long-term impact of development initiatives.

---

# Out of Scope

The current project does not aim to provide:

- a complete LMS
- a coaching platform
- performance management software
- competency management
- knowledge repositories
- certification programmes

These systems may integrate with habify30 but are not replaced by it.

---

# Relationship to Existing Learning

habify30 assumes that meaningful learning has already taken place.

Its purpose begins where most learning interventions end.

The platform focuses on the period during which participants attempt to translate intention into behaviour.
