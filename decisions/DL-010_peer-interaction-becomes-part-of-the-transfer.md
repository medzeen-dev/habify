# DL-010

## Decision

Peer interaction becomes part of the transfer architecture.

## Context

Behaviour change often remains invisible.

## Decision

Participants should not work entirely alone.

## Rationale

Peers provide:

- accountability
- encouragement
- perspective
- normalisation

These mechanisms strengthen implementation.
