# Canon

Version: 1.0
Status: Living Document

---

# Purpose

This document defines the immutable principles of habify30.

Every future product decision, feature request, marketing message, technical implementation or AI suggestion should be evaluated against these principles.

If a proposal violates one or more canonical principles, it should be challenged before implementation.

The Canon is intentionally short.

Everything else in the repository exists to support these principles.

---

# C-001

## habify30 is a behaviour change platform.

habify30 is not an e-learning platform.

Learning may be part of the experience.

Behaviour change is the outcome.

---

# C-002

## Behaviour is the product.

Knowledge is never the final product.

Understanding is valuable only if it increases the probability of sustained behavioural change.

---

# C-003

## Transfer is the competitive advantage.

The world already contains enough learning platforms.

habify30 exists because knowledge rarely transfers into everyday behaviour.

Everything should strengthen transfer.

Nothing should distract from it.

---

# C-004

## Habits beat motivation.

Motivation fluctuates.

Habits survive fluctuations.

The product therefore invests in systems rather than inspiration.

---

# C-005

## Tiny changes create lasting change.

Large transformations emerge from small, repeatable behaviours.

The platform should always reduce activation energy.

---

# C-006

## Simplicity wins.

Every additional step reduces completion.

Every unnecessary decision creates friction.

Whenever two solutions achieve similar outcomes, the simpler solution should be preferred.

---

# C-007

## Everyday life is the learning environment.

Real change does not happen inside the platform.

Real change happens during ordinary life.

habify30 exists to support behaviour in real contexts.

---

# C-008

## The platform should become invisible.

Success is not measured by platform usage.

Success is measured by behaviour that continues without the platform.

The ideal customer eventually no longer needs habify30.

---

# C-009

## Accountability supports change.

Behaviour changes more reliably when it becomes socially visible.

Peer structures should therefore increase commitment without creating pressure or shame.

---

# C-010

## The user owns the change.

habify30 never changes people.

People change themselves.

The product creates conditions that make change easier.

---

# C-011

## Coaches enable.

They do not rescue.

The role of a coach is to facilitate behaviour change rather than creating dependency.

---

# C-012

## Science before trends.

Methods should primarily be selected because they are supported by evidence.

Popularity is not sufficient.

---

# C-013

## Commercial success must follow user success.

Business incentives should align with behavioural outcomes.

Revenue should increase because users achieve lasting results.

Not because engagement is artificially maximised.

---

# C-014

## habify30 should integrate.

It should complement existing ecosystems.

It should not require organisations to replace successful systems.

---

# C-015

## Technology serves behaviour.

Technology is never implemented because it is technically interesting.

Every feature must increase the probability of sustained behaviour change.

---

# C-016

## AI augments coaching.

Artificial intelligence should strengthen reflection, guidance and personalisation.

It should not replace meaningful human relationships where they create value.

---

# C-017

## Every element must answer one question.

How does this increase the probability that the desired behaviour happens tomorrow?

If no convincing answer exists, the element should be reconsidered.

---

# C-018

## habify30 is a long-term system.

The objective is not a successful month.

The objective is lasting behavioural identity.

Every design decision should favour long-term sustainability over short-term excitement.

---

# C-019

## habify30 has a hard scope boundary, communicated clearly before commitment.

Addiction-related and therapy-requiring change goals are explicitly out of scope. habify30 is an implementation and habit programme; it does not replace medical or psychotherapeutic treatment.

This boundary is enforced through clear communication, not technical access control. The Ready Check gives participants an explicit, honest recommendation before they commit to the programme; a corresponding disclaimer also appears within the main programme itself, to set expectations rather than to gate access. Participants who are not a fit are told so directly — the goal is an informed decision, not a diluted version of the programme.

---

# C-020

## No profiling.

habify30 personalises only from a single, coarse, self-chosen signal.
It never derives risk profiles or automated individual decisions from combined participant signals (Art. 22 GDPR).
Personalisation serves the participant; it never becomes assessment of the participant.

*(Established by DL-075.)*

---

# Decision Filter

Every future proposal should be evaluated using the following questions.

1. Does it increase behavioural transfer?

2. Does it reduce friction?

3. Does it strengthen habits?

4. Does it support autonomy?

5. Does it fit everyday life?

6. Is it scientifically defensible?

7. Does it improve commercial sustainability?

8. Would removing it make the product weaker?

If several answers are "no", the proposal should be challenged.
